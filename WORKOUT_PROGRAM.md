# SanaFit — Weekly Home Workout Program

Goal: gain strength, muscle, and healthy body weight. Equipment: small and long resistance bands, 2.5 kg dumbbell(s), yoga ball, mat, chair, and wall.

Schedule: Saturday — Lower Body + Glutes; Sunday — Rest; Monday — Upper Body + Core; Tuesday — Rest; Wednesday — Full Body + Glutes; Thursday — Rest / light walking; Friday — Full Rest.

## Warm-up before every workout

- Walk in place — 2 minutes
- Shoulder circles — 30 seconds
- Hip hinges — 10 reps
- Bodyweight squats — 10 reps
- Arm swings + ankle movements — 1 minute

## Progression

Repeat the same workout. Add reps gradually while keeping your form controlled. When every set reaches the top of its range comfortably, make it harder with more band tension, slower reps, greater range, a harder variation, or heavier dumbbells. The last 2–3 reps should feel challenging without losing control.

## Rest guidance

- Rest 60–90 seconds between most sets.
- Rest up to 90–120 seconds after squats, lunges, and Romanian deadlifts.

## Weekly home plan

### Saturday — Lower body + glutes

1. Goblet Chair Squat — 3 × 8–12 reps; rest 90–120 sec.
2. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 10–12 reps; rest 90–120 sec.
3. Mini-Band Glute Bridge — 3 × 12–15 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
4. Supported Reverse Lunge — 2 × 8–10 reps each side; rest 90–120 sec.
5. Mini-Band Lateral Walk — 2 × 10–12 steps each direction; rest 60–90 sec.
6. Standing Calf Raise — 3 × 12–20 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
7. Yoga-Ball Wall Squat Hold — 2 × 20–30 seconds; rest 60–90 sec.

### Sunday — Rest

Rest day.

### Monday — Upper body + core

1. Long-Band Seated Row — 3 × 10–15 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
2. Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 3 × 8–12 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
3. Wall Push-Up — 3 × 8–15 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
4. Long-Band Biceps Curl — 3 × 10–15 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
5. Long-Band Triceps Extension — 2 × 10–15 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
6. Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 2 × 10–12 reps each side; rest 60–90 sec.
7. Dead Bug — 2 × 8–10 reps each side; rest 60–90 sec.
8. Yoga-Ball Seated Balance — 2 × 30–45 seconds; rest 60–90 sec.

### Tuesday — Rest

Rest day.

### Wednesday — Full body + glutes

1. Dumbbell Squat — 3 × 10–12 reps; rest 90–120 sec.
2. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 10–12 reps; rest 90–120 sec.
3. Long-Band Row — 3 × 10–15 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
4. Wall or Counter Push-Up — 3 × 8–12 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
5. Single-Leg Glute Bridge — 2 × 8–10 reps each side; rest 60–90 sec.
6. Mini-Band Standing Leg Abduction — 2 × 10–15 reps each side; rest 60–90 sec.
7. Dumbbell Biceps Curl — 2 × 10–12 reps; rest 60–90 sec.
8. Bird Dog — 2 × 8–10 reps each side; rest 60–90 sec.
9. Yoga-Ball Wall Squat — 2 × 10–12 reps; rest 60–90 sec.

### Thursday — Rest / light walking

Rest or optional light walking.

### Friday — Full rest

Full rest.

Workout length: approximately 30–45 minutes.

Progression: keep the same plan each week. Gradually add reps. When all sets reach the top of the range comfortably with good form, use more band tension, slower repetitions, greater range of motion, a harder variation, or eventually heavier dumbbells. The final 2–3 reps should feel challenging while technique stays controlled.

Safety: stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual breathing difficulty. Pair the workout with the food program and enough daily calories and protein for muscle and weight gain.

Attendance in the app records completed workout days locally.
